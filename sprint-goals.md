# Sprint Goals

*Instructions: For each sprint, write a one or two sentence goal plus the single demoable outcome that proves the sprint succeeded. Keep in mind that you will conduct user studies after sprints 1 and 2 to get feedback to inform future development, so define MVP goals to be most helpful for that. The sprint 3 MVP should be your final product.*

## Sprint 1
**Goal:**
Have a website hosted online, where customers and managers can log in. 

**Outcome:**
A user can create an account and choose whether they are a customer and manager. Once they do, they can view the respective WIP menus, even after logging out and logging in.
Managers can view a table of current inventory.
## Sprint 2
**Goal:**
Complete the basic functionality for both customers and managers.

**Outcome:**
Customers can view menu items, create and edit and order, and make a "payment"
Managers can view a restock report, a primitive prediction model, and a sales report.
## Sprint 3
**Goal:**
Implement advanced functionality, such as predictive inventory counts, and translation feature
**Outcome:**
Customers can view the menu in at least 5 languages.
Managers can access the new predictive model
